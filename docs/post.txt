Ask for Mac. Free, open source.

Your Mac can't do this on its own: Spotlight finds files but doesn't read them, Siri doesn't answer from your documents, and a chatbot wants you to upload them first.

Ask your Mac a question in your own words: "how much was the lease deposit", "email from Sam about the roof last week", "dentist invoice in March". Get the answer, with the file it came from, one click away.

Nothing leaves the Mac. Spotlight finds the files, the Mac reads them, and on macOS 26 Apple's on-device model writes the answer with citations. Without Apple Intelligence it quotes the sentence instead of writing one. When your files don't have the answer, it says so.

No account, no upload, no second index of your life on someone's server. Also a command line: askmac "tax return 2025 total"
